## How Samples Were Generated

All samples are **synthetic** -- written by hand or generated programmatically:

1. **Safe samples** (`safe_holdout.jsonl`): Generated across 5 categories (instructional,
   code, customer_support, creative_writing, technical_docs) to represent realistic
   production-like text that should NOT trigger prompt injection detection, including
   benign uses of injection trigger words as hard negatives.

2. **Malicious samples** (`malicious_holdout.jsonl`): Attack patterns across 13 Na0S
   taxonomy categories (D1-D8, E1, E2, C1, O1, P1). Templates use combinatorial
   generation (verb x target x payload) for D1; named-role templates for D2; structural
   markers for D3; encoding transforms for D4; Unicode substitutions for D5; multilingual
   phrasings for D6; multi-step/fragmented payloads for D7; context-flooding for D8.

3. **Adversarial evasion** (`adversarial_evasion.jsonl`): 63 core malicious prompts
   transformed through 9 evasion functions. Each sample retains the `original` field
   for traceability.

## Important Notes

- These datasets are **holdout** sets -- they must NOT be used for training or threshold tuning.
- The safe holdout is designed to measure **false positive rate (FPR)**.
- The malicious holdout measures **recall** (true positive rate).
- The adversarial evasion dataset measures **evasion resistance**.
- All data is deterministically generated with `random.seed(42)` for reproducibility.
- No external datasets (HuggingFace, PINT, etc.) are included; all content is original.
